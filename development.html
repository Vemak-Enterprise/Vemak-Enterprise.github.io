Project planning

A concise brief is enough to begin.

What should I include in a project brief?+

Describe the problem, intended users, essential features, current systems, preferred launch date and indicative budget. Share any integration or security requirements.

Can you work with existing systems?+

We can discuss improvements and integrations with an existing environment. Access, documentation and a technical review help establish the available options.

Do you offer hosting and maintenance?+

Cloud deployment and ongoing support can be considered within the proposal. Hosting costs, service coverage and responsibilities are agreed for each engagement.

How is a project priced?+

Pricing follows the agreed scope, complexity, integrations and delivery stages. Contact VEMAK to discuss a quotation for your requirements.
