Before you visit

A little detail makes
a better service.

Useful answers for planning your order.

How much does cleaning cost?+

Ask the branch for a quote based on item type, quantity, fabric and condition. Corporate requirements are discussed against a defined scope.

How long will my order take?+

The branch will confirm a collection date for your order. Please discuss urgent deadlines before dropping off your items.

Can I arrange collection or delivery?+

Contact the Randburg branch to discuss your location and order. Availability, scheduling and any transport charges must be confirmed before booking.

Do you accept specialist or healthcare linen?+

Specialist linen requirements need a separate review of the item types, handling instructions, process requirements and facility suitability before an order can be accepted.
