Website information

A straightforward
approach to privacy.

This page explains what happens when you browse this website or contact us.

Browsing

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics trackers, advertising cookies, account registration or payment processing. The hosting provider may process technical information as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Enquiries

Our enquiry forms send your name, email address, selected service, organisation and message over HTTPS to FormSubmit, which processes the submission and emails it to sipho.makam@gmail.com. We use this information to respond to your enquiry. This website does not maintain a separate enquiry database. FormSubmit is a third-party service with its own privacy practices. You can also contact us using the direct email links.

External links

Links to the official Crisp & Clean website and Google Maps take you to external services with their own privacy practices.

Privacy questions

Contact sipho.makam@gmail.com for privacy enquiries relating to VEMAK. Please avoid including sensitive identification or financial documents in an initial enquiry.
